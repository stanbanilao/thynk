WORLD 03 / AUTOMATION & RPA

Give repetitive work to digital workers.

Rules-based browser, desktop, document and data workflows can be automated while keeping human checkpoints for exceptions and judgment.

01 / CAPTURE

Bring work in.

Files, forms, emails, records or batches enter a controlled process.

02 / VALIDATE

Check the rules.

Required data, statuses and conditions are checked before the next action.

03 / PROCESS

Do the repeatable work.

Systems are opened, information is moved and rules-based actions are completed.

04 / REVIEW

Surface exceptions.

Anything uncertain is routed to a person instead of being silently guessed.
