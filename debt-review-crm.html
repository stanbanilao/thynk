FIRSTHAND INDUSTRY UNDERSTANDING / 2017—2024

Software should follow the business.

Thynkverse draws on hands-on experience in South African debt-review operations: intake, checking, handoffs and aftercare. That experience informs our approach; it is not a claim of endorsement by any former employer.

DIGITAL WORKER

Repeat what is known.

Follow tested rules, move through known steps, record the result and produce structured output.

HANDOFF
HUMAN REVIEW

Decide what needs judgement.

Review incomplete or unusual records and every decision that should remain with an authorised person.

HUMAN-LED / AUTOMATION-ASSISTED

Let digital workers handle repeatable steps. Keep the decisions.

A useful automation follows defined rules, records the result and stops when a person needs to decide.
