Questions business owners ask

A practical starting point.

The first conversation should establish whether a tailored CRM, automation or simply a clearer workflow is the right answer.

Do we have to replace our current debt-review software?

Not necessarily. We start by understanding your existing applications, processes and permissions. Some opportunities are better suited to an integration or a focused workflow tool. Compatibility is assessed before making commitments.

Can the CRM follow our company's own stages and roles?

That is the intended approach. We map your actual workspaces, approvals, access rules and exception handling first. A final specification and demonstration confirm which parts are ready for your environment.

Can the system automate every 17.1 or NCR-related task?

No blanket promise is made. Each automation is evaluated against the platform's rules, available access and your compliance needs. Where professional judgement or authorisation is required, the person responsible stays in control.

What should we bring to an initial demo?

A typical workflow and a description of where staff lose time are enough. Please do not send identity numbers, financial records, account credentials or other confidential customer information in your initial enquiry.
